周五的下午在图书馆，真的没办法自习。

外面轰隆隆的不知在干嘛，一直吵到把图书馆里差不多一半的人都赶走了，我这才下定决心：走人！

四点了，回来自然没心情看书，打开计算机随便点了一 部G盘movie 文件夹里的没看过的电影，《冷山》，一个人就这么坐下来，哪知一坐就是三个小时！

从来没有看电影看得让我这么难受的 ，never ever a film could do me kind of sorrow and sadness like this. 

Unbelievably,I can even hardly breathe when "the bomb explodes just beneath those innocent people", when "Inman was shot nearly to dead and was lying on the patient bed to moan Cold Mountain...", when "the black figure of Inman finally fell down on the brightly shining snowfiled which was previewed by Ada a long time before". 

well,thank Kiddy for recommending me with this moving and specific movie to me,although I downed it without watching until this afternoon. 

However,I cannot point out one single reason that I like this movie,bcoz I am just feeling tough and sorrowful even till now,that's a difficult work to describe what I have been and am experiencing during my past three hours. 

In addition,landscape in Carolina attracts me a lot and it reminds me of my hometown-Longquan,Yichang,which shares a magnificent scenery in China with that in Carolina,US. 

Here is sth about Cold Mountain and its author- Charles Frazier . 

In fact,the novel is based on local history and stories handed down by Frazier's father about Frazier's great-great-uncle William Pinkney Inman.Inman, who was from the area around Cold Mountain in western North Carolina, served in the Confederate Army from which he deserted after being wounded twice and is reputedly buried in a local cemetery. The real Inman served as a private in Company F of the 25th North Carolina Infantry, and his regiment did participate in the fighting in the Siege of Petersburg , including the Battle of the Crater .
